E-invoice to JSON

Drop an e-invoice you received. It is unwrapped, identified and mapped to one canonical model built on EN 16931. You get the JSON, and the invoice rebuilt from it so you can check the mapping.

Nothing leaves this tab. The conversion runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.
