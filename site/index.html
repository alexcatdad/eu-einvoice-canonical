E-invoice to JSON

Drop an e-invoice you received. It is unwrapped, identified and mapped to one canonical model built on EN 16931, and you get the JSON.

Everything runs in this browser tab. Your file is never uploaded.

Paste XML instead

What it reads

Full conversion to canonical JSON: Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 and self-billing, Peppol PINT, XRechnung (UBL and CII), Factur-X and ZUGFeRD 2 in every profile, NLCIUS, CIUS-RO, CIUS-PT, HR-CIUS, OIOUBL, plain EN 16931, and Svefaktura 1.0.

Detected and identified, with parsers still to come: FatturaPA, KSeF FA(2)/FA(3), Facturae, ISDOC, ebInterface, Finvoice, TEAPPSXML, e-arve, eSLOG and myDATA.

Wrappers it opens: Factur-X PDF attachments, CAdES .p7m (binary or base64), ZIP archives, Peppol SBDH, SOAP envelopes, and legacy encodings such as ISO-8859-1.
