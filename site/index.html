Lines that talk about other lines

Invoices often mix real items with headings, notes and surcharge lines such as “10% material markup”. The converter marks each line as an item, heading, description or surcharge and links them, using rules and exact arithmetic. An optional language model that runs in your browser can label what the rules leave open; its answers are always marked for review.

What it reads

Peppol BIS 3 from every EU country, and national formats such as XRechnung, Factur-X, FatturaPA and KSeF. It also opens PDF attachments, signed .p7m files, ZIP archives, Peppol SBDH and SOAP envelopes.
